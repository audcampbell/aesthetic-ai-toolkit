# Build your provider pay plan

A free Claude skill for med spas, dermatology and plastic surgery practices, by Audrey Campbell, MPH ([The Audrey Aesthetic](https://theaudreyaesthetic.com)). Last reviewed November 2026.

Build a fair pay plan and written offer for an injector (RN, NP or PA), esthetician or wellness provider. The plan is built to keep good providers: a market base rate, a monthly KPI bonus paid in flat dollars, a clear path to raises, and real investment in their training. Claude checks what it all costs against the revenue the provider brings in.

## How to use it

1. Install it (below).
2. In Claude, say "help me build a pay plan for an injector in [city]."
3. Answer a few questions: the role, their license and experience, what they can do in your state, your starting rate, and what you want to invest in them.
4. Set goals for six KPIs: rebooking rate, consult conversion rate, memberships sold, average ticket, retail sales and 5-star reviews that name the provider.
5. Get the plan, the cost check and a written offer to review with your attorney.

## What you get back

- A base rate, with the market range and sources
- A monthly KPI scorecard: goals, stretch goals and points, paid in flat dollars
- A level ladder for raises at 6 and 18 months
- Retention extras (continuing education, conferences, training) and what they cost
- A cost table: monthly cost at no bonus, at goal and at stretch, as a share of the provider's revenue
- A written offer, as a draft
- A 90-day check-in script
- What to check with a healthcare attorney in your state

## Why no commission by default

How licensed providers can be paid on revenue varies by state, and bonuses change how overtime is figured for hourly employees. This skill builds bonuses as flat dollars for KPIs the provider controls. If you want commission, it tells you to have a healthcare attorney review it first, and offers to connect you with Audrey, who can refer attorneys she's worked with who specialize in medical aesthetics pay.

## Install the skill

- **Claude:** turn on file creation under Settings, Capabilities. Then go to Customize, Skills, +, Upload a skill, and pick `build-your-provider-pay-plan.zip` from the `downloads` folder.
- **Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add audcampbell/aesthetic-ai-toolkit`, then `/plugin install build-your-provider-pay-plan@aesthetic-ai-toolkit`.
- **ChatGPT or any other AI:** paste `SKILL.md` into a Project's instructions or at the top of a new chat.
