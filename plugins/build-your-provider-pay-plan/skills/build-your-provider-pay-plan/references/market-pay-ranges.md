# Market pay ranges for med spa providers

Checked November 19, 2026. These are directional. Most come from job postings and recruiting sites, not surveys of what providers actually earn, and pay varies a lot by city, license and experience. Show the owner the range and the source. Never present it as what they must pay.

## Nurse injectors (mostly RNs)

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| Aesthetic Hires, first-party data, Q3 2026 | Median posted pay about $55 an hour, across 117 employers hiring nurse injectors |
| Medical Spa Salary Guide 2026 (medsparecruiting.com) | Base of $40 to $65 an hour is common, often with commission on top |
| Aesthetic Education, 2026 | $41 to $72 an hour |
| Indeed, all med spas | Average about $49 an hour for nurse injectors |
| ZipRecruiter, 2026 | Average about $39 an hour for medical spa nurses |

**Working range to show owners:** about $40 to $65 an hour base, median about $55. NPs, PAs, very experienced injectors and major metros often sit above it. An owner starting at $75 an hour is paying at the top of the market, which can make sense for an NP or an injector with a following.

## Estheticians

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| Aesthetic Hires, first-party data, Q3 2026 | Median posted pay about $25 an hour, across 191 employers |

Pay rises with medical-grade services the state allows an esthetician to do. Check scope with the state board first.

## Wellness providers

No reliable posted-pay data found for med spa wellness roles. Use the range for the provider's license (RN, NP or PA) and adjust for the services they run.

## How to place someone in the range

- 0 to 2 years in the role: low end
- 3 to 5 years: middle
- 6 or more years, or a loyal patient following: high end
- Add for NP or PA licensure, a major metro, or added skills (wellness, devices)
