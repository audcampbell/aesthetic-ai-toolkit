# Retention extras checklist

Ask about each. Put a yearly dollar figure on every one so the owner sees the real cost, and the provider sees the real value.

## Growth
- Continuing education budget: $____ a year (courses, trainings, CE credits)
- Conferences: ____ a year. Does the practice pay registration, travel and the days off?
- New skills the practice will pay to train (a new device, wellness, advanced injection techniques). Is there a stay agreement after paid training? Have an attorney review it.
- A say in which services they grow into

## Time and benefits
- Paid time off: ____ days
- Health benefits
- Schedule flexibility (set days, a protected admin block)

## Product and treatments
- Treatment or product allowance for themselves: $____ a year
- Staff pricing on skincare

## The level ladder
- Level 1 to Level 2: after 6 months, hitting at least 4 KPI goals in 3 of the last 4 months. Raise: $____ an hour.
- Level 2 to Level 3: after 12 more months at the same standard, plus a new skill or certification. Raise: $____ an hour.

## Review rhythm
- 90-day check-in
- 6-month review (first raise decision)
- Every year after

## Three questions for every review
1. Which KPI are you proudest of, and which one is hardest right now?
2. What's getting in your way that we can fix?
3. What do you want to learn next?
