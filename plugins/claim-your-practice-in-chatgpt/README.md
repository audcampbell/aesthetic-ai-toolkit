# Claim your practice in ChatGPT

A free guide and AI skill for US med spas, dermatology and plastic surgery practices, by Audrey Campbell, MPH ([The Audrey Aesthetic](https://theaudreyaesthetic.com)). Last reviewed November 2026.

ChatGPT is already describing your practice to patients. This skill helps you:

1. **See what ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity say now.** That's your before picture.
2. **Claim your listing in ChatGPT Business Hub**, following OpenAI's own steps: verify by phone, email or domain, then confirm your identity with Persona.
3. **Fix wrong details** in your profile and at their sources: your website, Google Business Profile and directories.
4. **Write a profile description** with no claims you can't back up.
5. **Track mentions, website clicks and discovery topics** each month.

Claiming doesn't change where you rank in ChatGPT. What the rest of the web says about you does. Business Hub is in beta, so check OpenAI's help center ("Setting up your business on ChatGPT") for the current steps. Not affiliated with OpenAI.

## Install

- **Claude:** upload this folder as a zip under Customize, Skills.
- **Claude Code:** `/plugin install claim-your-practice-in-chatgpt@aesthetic-ai-toolkit`.
- **ChatGPT or any other AI:** paste `SKILL.md` into a Project's instructions or a new chat.
