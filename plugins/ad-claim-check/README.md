# Ad Claim Check

A free AI skill for US med spas, dermatology and plastic surgery practices, by Audrey Campbell, MPH ([The Audrey Aesthetic](https://theaudreyaesthetic.com)). Last reviewed November 2026.

Paste an ad, an Instagram caption, a website page, an email or a review reply. It flags lines the FTC, the FDA, your state medical board, or Meta and Google might flag, explains each rule in plain words, and rewrites the risky lines in your voice.

**Educational screening for US marketing, not legal advice.** It doesn't guarantee compliance or ad approval. Rules vary by state and change often.

## What it checks

1. **Proof behind claims (FTC):** "guaranteed," "permanent," "painless," "no downtime," "safe," "#1."
2. **Testimonials and influencers (FTC Endorsement Guides, 2023):** typical results, and disclosing free or paid treatment.
3. **Reviews (FTC rule, October 2024):** fake, bought or incentivized reviews, staff reviews, and hidden negatives.
4. **Before-and-after photos:** editing, lighting and pose, identifying models, naming the procedure, and patient permission.
5. **FDA status and brand names:** cleared versus approved, off-label uses, BOTOX® Cosmetic trademark use, and compounded GLP-1 claims.
6. **State board rules:** "board certified," titles, superiority claims, all-in prices and testimonials, with California, Texas, Florida, New York and Indiana examples.
7. **Meta and Google:** age targeting, negative self-image wording, transformation photos, and drug-term rules.
8. **Patient privacy:** written authorization for photos and stories, and review replies that don't confirm someone is a patient.

## Install

- **Claude:** turn on file creation under Settings, Capabilities. Then go to Customize, Skills, +, Upload a skill, and pick this folder as a zip.
- **Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add audcampbell/aesthetic-ai-toolkit`, then `/plugin install ad-claim-check@aesthetic-ai-toolkit`.
- **ChatGPT:** create a Project and paste `SKILL.md` into its instructions.
- **Any other AI:** paste `SKILL.md` at the top of a new chat, then paste your ad.

## Before you use it

- Never paste patient names, photos or stories.
- Check each product's FDA status and indication in the FDA databases.
- Have a healthcare advertising attorney review campaigns before they run.

The skill draws on:
- the FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance (2022), the Endorsement Guides (2023) and the consumer reviews rule (16 CFR Part 465, 2024)

- California Bus. & Prof. Code §651, Texas 22 TAC §164.3, Florida F.A.C. 64B8-11.001 and New York Educ. Law §6530(27)
- Meta's health and wellness ad policy, and Google's healthcare and medicines policy
- HIPAA, 45 CFR 164.508
