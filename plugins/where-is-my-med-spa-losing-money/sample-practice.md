# Sample med spa (made up)

Every number is made up to show how the diagnostic works.

## Last month

| Number | Value |
|---|---|
| Service revenue | $120,000 |
| Completed appointments | 240 |
| Lost to no-shows and late cancels | 36 |
| Provider hours scheduled / booked | 480 / 230 |
| New inquiries / booked | 180 / 63 |
| Patients who rebooked | 25% |
| Discounts given | $9,000 |
| Retail sales | $6,000 |

Average ticket: $500. Revenue per booked hour: $521.74.

## Leaks at the starting goals, ranked

| Leak | You | Goal | Worth a month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rebooking | 25% | 50% | $30,000 in future visits |
| Inquiries that never book | 35% | 50% | $13,500 |
| Appointments lost | 13.0% | at most 8% | $6,960 |
| Retail | 5% of services | 10% | $6,000 in sales |
| Discounts | 7.0% | at most 5% | $2,550 |
| Open hours (shown separately) | 48% booked | 56% | 38 hours, about $20,243 |

**The finding:** rebooking is the biggest leak. Fix it first, then inquiries. The leaks overlap, so don't add them up.

## Where the starting goals come from

These are goals I chose to start from, not industry averages. Change them.
- **Schedule booked, 56%:** Zenoti's 2026 med spa benchmark, from its own customers' 2025 data, put the 75th percentile for staff utilization at 56% and the median at 38%.
- **Appointments lost, 8%:** the same source put no-shows at 4–5% and cancellations at 14%. This goal combines no-shows and late cancellations.
- **The rest:** owner-set starting points.
