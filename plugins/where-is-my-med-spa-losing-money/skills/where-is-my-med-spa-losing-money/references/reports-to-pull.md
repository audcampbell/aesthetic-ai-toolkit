# Which reports to pull, and where

Last checked: November 2026, from each vendor's own help center or website. Menu names change, so tell the owner to confirm in their live system. "Not verified" means no official source was found: give the generic report name and tell them to search their system's help center or ask their rep. Never guess a menu path.

## What each report gives you (the "Need" numbers in the tables below)

| Need | Report type | Gives these of the ten numbers (KPIs) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales summary | Service revenue, retail sales (retail as a share of service revenue) |
| 2 | Appointments, cancellations and no-shows | Completed appointments, appointments lost (lost-appointment rate) |
| 3 | Rebooking or pre-booking | Share of patients who left with their next visit booked |
| 4 | Utilization or staff performance | Provider hours scheduled and booked (schedule booked %) |
| 5 | Discounts | Discounts and promotions given (discounts as a share of gross revenue) |
| 6 | Leads, sources or online booking | New inquiries and how many booked (inquiry booking rate). Most systems don't track calls and DMs; if so, ask for the count from the front desk log or phone system. |
| 7 | Memberships | Not one of the ten, but useful: active members, cancellations |
| 8 | Sales by provider | Not one of the ten, but useful: revenue per provider |

When you give an owner their list, show only their system, in this shape: report name, where it lives, set the date range to last month, export as summary CSV (or PDF), and which numbers it gives.

## General tips
- Set the date range to the **last full calendar month** (1st to last day), and use the same range on every report.
- Choose **summary/aggregate** views, not patient-level detail. Don't export PHI the analysis doesn't need.
- Export as **CSV** if the report offers it (easiest to analyze), and use PDF otherwise.
- Check whether the report uses **transaction date or appointment/service date** (Vagaro's Sales Summary offers both). Also check whether it is **accrual or cash** (Zenoti), and whether it shows **memberships, packages and gift cards at sale or at redemption** (Mangomint shows them at redemption).
- Close out or check out every appointment before exporting. Unclosed tickets drop out of sales reports (Vagaro's Missed Checkouts report exists for this reason).
- Note whether figures include or exclude tax and tips.
- If a dashboard metric can't be exported, take a screenshot that shows the date range.
