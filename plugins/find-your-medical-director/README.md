# Find and vet your medical director

A free kit for US med spa owners and nurse injectors, by Audrey Campbell, MPH ([The Audrey Aesthetic](https://theaudreyaesthetic.com)). Last reviewed November 2026.

What a medical director actually does, the questions to ask on the first call, a scorecard to compare candidates, the red flags, what belongs in the agreement, and a checklist to bring to your healthcare attorney.

**This is general information, not legal advice.** Who can own a med spa, who can be its medical director and how closely they must supervise all vary by state, and the rules are changing fast. Confirm everything with your state medical and nursing boards and a licensed healthcare attorney.

## The two stops

When you compare candidates, two answers end the conversation:

1. **Not licensed in your state.**
2. **Pay as a percentage of revenue.** Fair-market pay set in advance (flat or hourly) is the safer model. Percentage pay can be illegal fee-splitting in many states.
