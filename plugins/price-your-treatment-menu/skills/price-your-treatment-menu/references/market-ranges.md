# Published price and cost ranges

Last checked: November 2026. Use these to sanity-check a price, never to set it. The owner's price comes from their own costs and target. Say the source and date whenever you cite a figure. **Strong** means a manufacturer label, a dataset with a stated method, or a society survey. **Directional** means vendor or clinic marketing.

## Patient prices: neurotoxin per unit
| Product | Published range | Strength | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Botox Cosmetic | $9 to $18, median $13; most practices between $12 and $14 | Strong | Pabau price check of 562 practices' published prices, Nov 2026 |
| Xeomin, Jeuveau | median $12 | Strong | same |
| Daxxify | median $12, middle range $8 to $14 | Strong | same |
| Dysport | median $4.66 per Dysport unit | Strong | same |
| Letybo | $9 to $13 | Directional | Portrait Care, Nov 2026 |

**By market type:** the Pabau data found the same median ($13) in metro and non-metro practices. Higher big-city ranges ($18 to $25) appear only in vendor guides. Treat a price above about $15 a unit as a positioning choice (a luxury brand or a physician injector), not something the market sets. No sourced rural figure exists.

**Positioning tiers (directional, Zenoti, July 2026):** value $9 to $12, mid-market $13 to $16, premium $17 to $25 per Botox unit.

**Daxxify and Dysport per unit:** Audrey's recommendation is that the price per unit follows the unit ratio, so an area costs about the same with any toxin (Botox $14 a unit means Daxxify about $7; Botox $15 means Dysport about $5). The published data shows many practices instead charge Daxxify about the same per unit as Botox (median $12 against $13, Pabau 2026), which makes a Daxxify area cost about twice as much. If an owner prices that way, show the per-area gap.

## Patient prices: per area and filler
- Neurotoxin per area: about $250 to $350 an area, full-face sessions around $800 (directional, Zenoti 2026). ASPS 2023 average neuromodulator fee: $435 (strong).
- HA filler per syringe: about $550 to $1,200 (directional). ASPS 2023 average for injectable lip augmentation: $698 (strong). Most practices price per syringe.
- Radiesse: about $600 to $800 a syringe (RealSelf, patient-reported). Sculptra: about $800 to $1,000 a vial, often 2 to 4 vials (directional).

## Patient prices: skin and device (RealSelf patient-reported averages per treatment, Oct 2025; a "treatment" may be a series)
Microneedling $848 · RF microneedling (Genius) $957 · Morpheus8 $1,889 · IPL $770 · chemical peel $413 · HydraFacial about $207 to $225 · laser hair removal $1,047 (usually a series).

## Memberships (directional, Zenoti 2026)
$149 to $249 a month is typical, with 10% to 20% off injectables. Going past 15% to 20% off often puts member treatments below cost; run the member margin check.

## Dilution on the label
- Botox Cosmetic: 2.5 mL per 100 units (or 1.25 mL per 50), 4 units per 0.1 mL.
- Daxxify: 1.2 mL per 100 units (or 0.6 mL per 50), 8 units per 0.1 mL.
- Dysport: 10 units per 0.08 mL or per 0.05 mL (about 2.5 mL or 1.5 mL per 300-unit vial).
- Letybo: 4 units per 0.1 mL.
Every label says units are not interchangeable between products. The ratios in the skill are a clinical convention.

## Waste
Practices that don't track usage lose about 10% to 16% of product (directional, Zenoti 2026). Use 5% for practices that track closely.
