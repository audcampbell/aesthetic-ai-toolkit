# Sample practice (made up)

Every number here is made up to show how the tool works. A few defaults are based on published figures, listed under "Where the starting numbers come from". Use your own invoices.

## Practice

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Room and facility costs a month | $15,000 |
| Team and admin costs a month | $25,000 |
| Treatment rooms, hours open each | 3, 180 hours |
| Providers, hours each | 3, 160 hours |
| How full the schedule is | 50% |
| Provider cost per hour | $70 |
| Card fees | 3% |
| Discounts and loyalty | 5% |
| Patients who finance, and the fee | 10%, 6% |
| Commission | 0% |
| Profit wanted on each treatment | 20% |

- Each booked room hour carries **$55.56** of facility costs.
- Each booked provider hour carries **$104.17** of team costs.
- The practice keeps **91.4%** of each dollar.

**The finding:** the signature facial loses $82.84 every time it's booked. It holds a room and a provider for an hour, and that hour carries $159.73 of fixed costs before any product is used.

## Where the starting numbers come from

These sources were checked in November 2026. Replace every figure with your own.

- **Neurotoxin cost of $6.20 a unit:** below AbbVie's published list price for Botox Cosmetic of $656 per 100-unit vial (February 2024). Practices often pay less after rebates.
- **5% waste:** small studies found about 5 units lost per 100-unit vial when drawing through the stopper.
- **Filler at $425 a syringe:** near distributor list prices for hyaluronic acid filler.
- **A schedule 50% full:** Zenoti's 2026 med spa benchmark, from its own customers' 2025 data, put median staff utilization at 38% and the 75th percentile at 56%.
- **$70 an hour for a provider:** the US median pay for nurse practitioners was $132,300 a year in May 2025 (Bureau of Labor Statistics), about $64 an hour, plus payroll taxes.
- **6% financing fee:** an assumption. Published rates vary by lender and plan.
