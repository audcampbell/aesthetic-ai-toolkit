# Sample practice (made up)

Every number is made up to show how the tool works. A few defaults come from published figures, listed under "Where the starting numbers come from". Use your own invoices.

## Practice

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Market and positioning | Suburban Philadelphia, luxury |
| Profit wanted on each treatment | 35% |
| Room and facility costs a month | $15,000 |
| Team and admin costs a month | $25,000 |
| Rooms, hours open each | 3, 180 hours |
| Providers, hours each | 3, 160 hours |
| How full the schedule is | 50% |
| Provider cost per hour | $70 |
| Membership discount | 10% |
| Other discounts and loyalty | 5% |
| Card fees | 3% |
| Patients who finance, and the fee | 10%, 6% |
| Commission | 0% |

- Each booked room hour carries **$55.56** of facility costs.
- Each booked provider hour carries **$104.17** of team costs.
- The practice keeps **91.4%** of each dollar, and **86.8%** from a member.

## Results

| Treatment | How priced | Costs you | Suggested | Charges now | Margin now | Member margin | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Neurotoxin, upper face (Botox Cosmetic, 64 units) | Per area | $531.50 | $945 (about $15 a unit) | $1,050 | 40.8% | 36.1% | On target |
| Neurotoxin, full face (64 + 30 units) | Per unit | $784.23 | $1,395, or $15 a unit | $14 a unit ($1,316) | 31.8% | 27.2% | Under your target |
| Cheek augmentation (1–2 syringes) | Range | $626.18 to $1,108.61 | $1,115 to $1,970 | $1,500 to $2,700 | 49.6% to 50.3% | 45.0% to 45.7% | On target |
| Lip filler (1 syringe, numbing) | Per area | $636.18 | $1,130 | $950 | 24.4% | 19.8% | Under your target |
| RF microneedling (numbing) | Per treatment | $420.28 | $750 | $1,100 | 53.2% | 48.6% | On target |
| BBL or IPL, full face | Per treatment | $134.12 | $240 | $450 | 61.6% | 57.0% | On target |
| Microneedling (numbing) | Per treatment | $253.96 | $455 | $285 | 2.3% | −2.3% | Loses money for members |

**The findings:**
- Microneedling at $285 makes a little from regular patients but loses money every time a member books it.
- Full-face neurotoxin at $14 a unit is under target. A per-area price of $1,395 fixes it.

## Where the starting numbers come from

These were checked in November 2026. Replace every figure with your own.

- **64 units for the upper face:** the FDA label for Botox Cosmetic (forehead 20, frown lines 20, crow's feet 24) and for Xeomin (2024).
- **Frown lines:** Botox Cosmetic, Xeomin, Jeuveau and Letybo 20; Dysport 50; Daxxify 40.
- **Other products' upper face:** 64 Botox units at common conversion ratios (Dysport about 3 to 1, Daxxify about 2 to 1, Jeuveau and Letybo about 1 to 1). These ratios are a clinical convention, not FDA doses.
- **$6.20 a unit:** below AbbVie's published list price for Botox Cosmetic of $656 per 100-unit vial (February 2024).
- **5% waste:** small studies found about 5 units lost per 100-unit vial when drawing through the stopper.
- **Filler at $425 a syringe:** near distributor list prices for hyaluronic acid filler.
- **A schedule 50% full:** Zenoti's 2026 med spa benchmark, from its customers' 2025 data, put median staff utilization at 38% and the 75th percentile at 56%.
- **$70 an hour:** the US median for nurse practitioners was $132,300 a year in May 2025 (Bureau of Labor Statistics), about $64 an hour, plus payroll taxes.
- **Profit targets by positioning, and the 6% financing fee:** starting assumptions to change.
